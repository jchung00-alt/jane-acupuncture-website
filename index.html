Meet Your Acupuncturist

Jane Chung

MSOM, L.Ac — Licensed Acupuncturist & Herbalist

With over 25 years of experience in traditional Korean medicine, Jane brings a unique blend of ancient wisdom and modern understanding to every patient encounter. She earned her Master of Science in Oriental Medicine and has dedicated her career to helping patients find lasting relief.

Jane believes that every patient deserves to be heard and understood. She takes the time to listen to your concerns, understand your health history, and develop a treatment plan that honors both your immediate needs and long-term wellness goals.
